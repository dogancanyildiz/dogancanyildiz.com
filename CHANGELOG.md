## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-27

Baseline of the rebuilt portfolio: phases 0 to 4 of the roadmap in
`docs/10-yol-haritasi.md`, merged one pull request per phase.

### Features

- **security and hygiene** ([#2]): security headers and a Content Security
  Policy in `next.config.ts`, `output: "standalone"`, the `/api/health` route,
  a hardened `/api/contact` handler with rate limiting and a honeypot field,
  and the `typecheck`, `test` and `format` scripts that the quality gate runs.
- **deploy pipeline** ([#3]): multi stage `Dockerfile` with a non root runner,
  `.dockerignore`, `docker-compose.yml` for local verification, the GitHub
  Actions quality gate, and the Coolify, Cloudflare, Traefik and Resend setup
  checklists under `docs/deploy/`.
- **internationalization** ([#4]): next-intl routing with English at the root
  and Turkish under `/tr`, the `[lang]` segment, the locale aware proxy, and
  the `messages/en.json` and `messages/tr.json` message catalogues.
- **design system** ([#5]): the design token layer, typography scale, motion
  primitives, layout shell, navigation with a Radix mobile menu, theme
  switching and the accessibility guarantees covered by unit tests.
- **content and launch** ([#6]): the Velite content pipeline with project and
  post schemas, case studies and blog posts in both locales, project and blog
  routes, per locale RSS feeds, the sitemap with hreflang alternates, JSON-LD
  and the real profile copy.

### Other

- Repository documentation: architecture decision records in `docs/`,
  executable phase plans in `docs/plans/`, and the handoff notes and manual
  checklists that go with them.
